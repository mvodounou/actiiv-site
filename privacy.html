Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Actiiv helps you plan and do short exercises. It handles health and activity information, so this page says plainly what it uses, where it stays, and what you control. The short version: your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud, we run no servers that receive it, and there are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics.

You can use Actiiv without allowing Health access and without signing in. You then get a starter plan based on your goal and level.

Subscriptions and payment

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. The app asks the App Store on your device whether you have an active subscription and keeps the date it ends on your device, so that reminders and nudges stop when it does.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not use your health data for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not include third-party analytics or advertising code in the app.

Things that leave your device

  • iCloud. Your settings, current weekly plan and today's plan are saved to Apple's iCloud key-value storage in your own iCloud account when iCloud is available. If you sign in with Apple, a new device restores them and your devices stay in sync. Your history is not saved to iCloud. Apple's privacy terms apply to that storage. We cannot read it.
  • The exercise list. The app may download an updated list of exercises from a server we operate (api.actiiv.app). This is a plain download. It sends no health data, plan or personal details, but, as with any web request, the server can see your IP address and basic request information. If the download fails, the app uses its built-in list.
  • Apple. Sign in with Apple, Apple Health, iCloud, notifications and Live Activities are Apple services and are governed by Apple's policies.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, nudges and the Dynamic Island exercise display are created on your phone. No health data is sent to a server to produce them. You can turn notifications and Live Activities off in iOS Settings at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Health access: change or withdraw it in the Health app under Sharing, or in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your data: in Actiiv, open Settings and tap Delete my data. It removes your plans, history, settings and Sign in with Apple from the iPhone and the copy in your iCloud. It does not cancel your subscription or delete anything from Apple Health. Deleting the app also removes its data from that device. To remove synced data, open iOS Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, and remove Actiiv's data.
  • Sign out: Settings in the app has a Sign Out button.
  • Access, correction, deletion: because we do not hold your data on our servers, most of this is in your hands on your device. If you believe we hold any information about you, contact us and we will help, including under the laws that apply where you live.

Children

Actiiv is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Health and safety

Actiiv gives general wellness and exercise suggestions. It is not medical advice or a medical device. See the terms.

Security

Data stays inside the app's protected storage on your device, and the Sign in with Apple identifier is held in the Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change how Actiiv handles data, we will update this page and the date above, and ask for your permission again where the law or Apple's rules require it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@actiiv.app.
